# Reforge YSWS Mock Website
Below lies a guide on how to use the features on the website.
## Home
### Computer
- Scroll Up & Down
- Drag the sword scrollbar on the right to jump through the page
- Scroll Down --> Sword moves into scabbard & glows near the bottom
- Hover on nav links, labels, buttons, and process cards --> Glow / lift effect
- Click anywhere --> Spark particles
- Press 🔥 / ⚫ --> Toggle dark mode
- Press RSVP or Learn More
### Mobile
- Scroll Up & Down
- Tap burger menu --> Open navigation
- Tap nav links to switch pages
- Tap anywhere --> Spark particles
- Tap 🔥 / ⚫ --> Toggle dark mode
- Tap RSVP or Learn More
- No hover effects
- No sword scrollbar on mobile

## FAQ
### Computer
- Scroll Up & Down
- Drag the sword scrollbar on the right
- Scroll Down --> Sword position updates and glows near bottom
- Hover on nav links and FAQ cards --> Glow

- Click another FAQ question --> Close the old one and open the new one
- Click anywhere --> Spark particles
- Press 🔥 / ⚫ --> Toggle dark mode
- FAQ shows in 2 columns on desktop
### Mobile
